    Tiny Demonlist

    A challenge list exclusively for tiny length levels!

    Website built by tFluffy and ThePvzProMaster; Original design from the LRR Demon List

    Reach out to us!

    If you would like to contact any of our staff members about the list, joining our server is the easiest way to do so. At least 1 staff member is online at all times, so any questions or concerns you have will be answered by someone! You also have to be in the server to submit levels or completions, so if you have one to submit, then join the server! Also, please read the list rules in the #list-standards channel before submitting a verification or completion.
